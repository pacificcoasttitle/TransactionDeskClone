Privacy & Data Protection

General

This statement is an extension to the privacy and security clause mentioned in the terms of service and hence general definitions as mentioned in terms of service may apply. This statement is a binding agreement between MyReposit (synonymous with www.myreposit.com) and users using the functionalities available on MyReposit. Users subscribing to services on MyReposit of any nature, hereby, consent to storage and processing of his/her personal data as mentioned in this statement of privacy and data protection.

MyReposit – helping researchers connect better and communicate faster.

Master data

  1. Definition of master data
    master data implies all data belonging to the user or person. This does not pertain to actual use of services available on MyReposit. Master data implies entities such as user email, name, gender, date of birth, research biodata, general of specific contact information, special information such as conferences attended and membership to groups within a network functional on MyReposit.
  2. Type and procedure of master data collected and stored on MyReposit
    MyReposit collects data as entered for fields of different kind of publications, its type and subcategories. Data entered in these fields is stored “as is” in addition to personal photographs, curriculum vitae/biodata and other published academic attachment(s) that are also submitted and stored on myreposit “as is”. Information on users entering and exiting groups and conferences, content of user blogs pertaining to one to more parties, and personal literature (synonymous with unpublished literature) after procuring user approvals is stored on MyReposit “as is”.
  3. Nature of master data processed by MyReposit
    MyReposit entails processing data as an integral part of its services. Users visiting a profile page on MyReposit see data that has been processed by MyReposit services. A basic entity such as user name is visible to all. However, options to make most of user data visible to select close friends or group exist. MyReposit utilizes user master data randomly to produce dynamic web pages. MyReposit may also use the master data to generate anonymous statistics although such statistics will not contain private information of individual users.
  4. Utilization of user email address
    MyReposit will process user email address solely to send emails that will notify users of services or change of services. MyReposit may insert attachments in the form of advertisements for products and services of any third party associated with MyReposit.
  5. Viewing, changing and deleting Master data
    users can view and edit their data from their profile pages. Users can also choose to delete all their master data except for their name, email address and password. If a user cancels his/her MyReposit subscription (please contact MyReposit by email, or postal mail if such is required), MyReposit will delete all User data. MyReposit as policy does not store personal data of Users who have unsubscribed from its services.

Traffic data

  1. Traffic data
    Traffic data implies data pertain to MyReposit usage. Traffic data is largely tracking information procured during period of log-in as well as information of duration and activities of use. Traffic data also pertains to IP address/es and information sent automatically by User web browser for example browser software, preferred languages, and the operating system being used.
  2. Nature of traffic collected and stored by MyReposit
    as mentioned earlier, MyReposit collects user traffic data and stores such data for the duration of user session (a session begins when users log in and ends when users log out). Each session is programmed to time out spontaneously after approx. One hour 60 of inactivity). At the end of a session, MyReposit, reserves the right to store traffic data for 24 hours. In such cases, user data is connected only to the user ip address and not to user account containing personal information. Although as policy, MyReposit does not attached name of user to traffic data, other entities such as user internet service provider might do so. After the duration of 24 hours, MyReposit will only store information on how many pages the user has viewed in 30 days (only numbers of pages are saved while information on which pages the user has viewed in not stored).
  3. Traffic data regulation
    In the course of active user sessions, MyReposit processes traffic data that is used in web services. Post sessions, MyReposit reserves the right to process data from traffic and incorporate the same to retrieve anonymous statistics. Such statistics is used to improve service and user experience. These statistics can also be used to process security threats and detection of fraud. Importantly, number of pages a user views gives an estimate of security measure and alerts team at MyReposit that the user might be intentionally trying to harvest data or in other words automatically trying to gain user’s personal data.
  4. Storing, changing and deleting traffic data
    should there be a need for user to view, change or delete traffic data, please contact MyReposit at help@myreposit.com. In cases where a personal profile account on MyReposit is removed, the user’s personal traffic data is automatically deleted.
  5. External tracking software use by MyReposit
    for visitors not logged in MyReposit uses google analytics (web analytics service operated by google, Inc., California, USA (“google”) services are used as external tracking software. Google analytics place cookies on user computers that help analyze how users use MyReposit services. Information obtained about a website by the use of cookies (including your IP address) is stored on google servers in the united states of America and the information is used to (a) evaluate the use of a website (b) to develop reports on the website activity for website operators, and (c) provide other services relating to website activity and internet usage. Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required and permitted to do so lawfully. You may choose not be tracked by google by setting an opt-out cookie; click here to do so. Users are requested to check Google’s terms and policies here for further information.

Cookies

  1. What are cookies?
    Cookies are little files stored by your web browser. Cookies are made when MyReposit sends request to your browser and when this request is accepted. Later, your web browser, on request, will send the cookie back to MyReposit.
  2. User preference for cookies
    Although cookies can increase the functionality by helping generate individual pages, MyReposit can be used without cookies. In most cases, cookies are switched on by default in MyReposit and on most web browsers. This default setting once switched off from privacy setting available on browsers, will stop sending cookies to MyReposit. Please refer to web browser help services for instructions on how to delete cookies on your computer and how to set web browsers to stop accepting cookies.
  3. About Data stored in cookies and processing such data
    Should Users choose to, they can agree to cookie during login, which enables the User to be automatically logged every time he/she visits MyReposit. This is a sensitive options and SHOULD NOT be used on public computers or even on private computers accessible to more than one person. Cookies store information on function within MyReposit and also of User pages. To retrieve such information, cookies can be processed randomly. MyReposit does not store cookie data or attempt’s to access cookie data from User network or groups.

Private messages

  1. Processing private messages
    MyReposit processes messages sent privately and delivers them to its respective recipients. MyReposit reserves the right to investigate and extract information from messages and/or from its attachments to screen for anti-spam and for virus threats. Threats from such messages (NOT content of messages) are stored permanently. As policy, MyReposit respects the privacy and confidentiality of its Users and therefore has made the process of processing private messages entirely automated. In such situations where security threat is detected or if a message is abusive in nature, the recipient of the message is the sole person cognizant of the threat.
  2. Sharing private messages with third parties
    MyReposit does not share private messages with any third party. However, in situations where legal obligations may warranty or such may be required of judicial authority, MyReposit might be obliged to share private messages with third parties.

Transmission of data

  1. Third parties processing data on behalf of MyReposit
    MyReposit reserves the right to seek services of third parties with respect to database or software development. For this it might become mandatory to share personal data and processed by third parties. In such circumstances, MyReposit is fully responsible for personal data of its Users and shall bind third parties to written agreement that adheres them to total compliance to MyReposit’s privacy and data protection clause. MyReposit is dedicated to selecting contractors who are trustworthy and reliable and respect privacy and data protection. MYREPOSIT DOES NOT SELL OR SHARE PERSONAL DATA WITH THIRD PARTIES.

Privacy and Data protection supervision

  1. Contact particulars in case of request or complaint about privacy or data protection
    As policy, MyReposit considers privacy and data protection as a vital issue as they directly impact the trust factor established between client and their service provider. MyReposit therefore considers privacy and data protection of its Users as a pivotal to the foundation and purpose of its creation and therefore allocates privacy and data protection issues the highest priority for resolution. For requests and complaints, please write to data protection in-charge Dr. Manish Joshi at manishjoshi@myreposit.com

Changes to statement on privacy and data protection

  1. Notifying users of changes in this statement on privacy and data protection?

    By using services on MyReposit, the user expressly agree to this statement on privacy and data protection.

    In situation of change or amendment of statement on privacy and data protection, user will be informed upon login and will be prompted to agree to amendment of statement on privacy and data protection. Myreposit does not make binding changes to statement on privacy and data protection or may do so only after consent has been obtained from the user.

    For further queries, please write to info@myreposit.com
