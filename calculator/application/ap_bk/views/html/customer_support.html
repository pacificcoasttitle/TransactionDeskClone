Customer Support

Do you have any questions about our services? If our Frequently Asked Questions page doesn't have the answer you are looking for, you can use this page to contact us directly. If you prefer to contact us via phone or using our live chat feature, please visit the Contact Details page.

Our support team is always available to help you with anything you need. We can send you a price quote, help you calculate your word count, show you how to use your AJE account, and answer any questions you have about our services.

Email us

Please use this form to send us your question. Our staff will respond to you with a detailed answer as quickly as possible.

If you already have an AJE account, please enter the e-mail address you use to log in. This will help us to process your question more quickly.
