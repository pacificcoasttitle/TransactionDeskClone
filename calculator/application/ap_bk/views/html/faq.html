What is MyReposit?

MyReposit is a scholarly collaborative platform that allows clinicians, and individual researchers deposit, disseminate and manage their content. MyReposit eases access to clinical and scientific information while at the same time helps scholars connect, collaborate and share data and views with peers and coauthors.

Why Choose MyReposit?

Users wishing to access MyReposit can do so ONLY after SIGNING UP.

Steps to SIGN UP

  1. Allows clinicians and researchers to host a variety of published and unpublished literature on a single platform.
  2. Supports and hosts open access published literature.
  3. Make your unpublished work visible and discoverable thorough persistent identifiers.
  4. Access smart article level metrics to measure the impact of scholarly work.
  5. No copyright transfers for author generated content.
  6. Sell and purchase ebooks and other customized items securely. Receive payments directly into your bank or via PayPal services.
  7. Check purchase history and access receipts from personal dashboard. Quick access to Hourly Transaction Reports.
  8. Purchase books and allied items at special rates.
  9. Connect with Societies and Events across the world.
  10. Network with colleagues, blog views to a wider academic audience.
  11. Notify the world of your work with Tweets and LinkedIn portals..

Getting started on MyReposit?

Users wishing to access MyReposit can do so ONLY after SIGNING UP.

Steps to SIGN UP

  1. Click on the SIGN UP tab on the right corner of the page.
  2. Fill the Sign up form details
  3. Submit details and verify account by clicking on link sent to your email.
  4. Your account will now be AUTHORIZED and you will be notified once this is done via email.
  5. Once your account has been authorized, you can login and access the content on MyReposit.

Creating user profile on MyReopsit

MyReposit works by creating USER PROFILE which allows customization of User environment with personal photograph and curriculum vitae/biodata. Through profiles, Users can upload and exhibit their pre-and post-print publications, books, book chapters, non-textual media content, conference proceedings, working papers, stories for the media etc. Viewer can download the content either freely or access them via pay-per-view/purchase options as set by the Author. MyReposit offers these features to Institutions and Societies as well.
