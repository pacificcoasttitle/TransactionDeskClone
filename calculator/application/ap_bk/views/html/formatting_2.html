Illustration and Graphing

In addition to formatting your existing figures, we can also assist you by generating original illustrations based on sketches, re-drawing low-resolution diagrams and flowcharts to generate publication quality figures, or creating graphs from data tables. Our illustrators work directly with authors to help you turn your ideas and data into figures that will help you communicate your research to the world, be it in print, posters, or presentations. Due to the unique nature of this service, pricing will vary based on the project, but you can see examples of our pricing in the before and after pictures below. Request your own custom quote!
