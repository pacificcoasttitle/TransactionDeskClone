How is this information used?

MyReposit uses the information you enter here to pre-fill submission forms so that you do not have to manually enter your name and other information each time you submit new content.

If you opt to receive download and usage counts, your email address will be used to deliver these reports to you once per month. We may occasionally send important announcements related to your publications or the eScholarship service.

Our usage of your information is further governed by the CDL Privacy Policy.
