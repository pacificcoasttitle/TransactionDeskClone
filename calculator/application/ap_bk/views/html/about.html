About MyReposit

What is MyReposit?

MyReposit is a scholarly collaborative platform that helps researchers connect better and communicate faster. Our mission is to help researchers share their publications, collaborate with peers, co-authors, and colleagues, find their research stats, ask and answer questions.

MyReposit – helping researchers connect better and communicate faster.

Advantages with MyReposit:

For Published Literature

  • Increase visibility and impact of your scholarly work
  • Gather publication impact through article level metrics
  • Create your profile
  • Share your research and follow other´s
  • Endorse skills of co-authors, and colleagues
  • Connect and collaborate with co-authors
  • Real-time public rating and comments on your work
  • Blog to a wider academic audience
  • Connect with Societies and Events across the world.
  • Become a Reviewer
  • Get critical feedback before submitting manuscript to a Journal
  • Partner with us to prepare your manuscript for publication
  • Request copy from author if not freely available
  • Search extensively by author, institution, journal, or publication
  • Ask questions, get answers
  • Seek jobs or recruitment
  • Write stories for the media
  • Purchase books and reading materials at special rates.
  • Sell your books and allied items

For Unpublished Literature

  • Clear demarcation of unpublished content from published research on your profile
  • Expert Scorecard - Critical feedback, scores and comments on content from three experts in your field
  • Maximum visibility with real-time public rating and comments on your content
  • Editing, formatting and layout services
  • Plagiarism check on content
  • Persistent online identifiers for better discoverability
  • Option of open or paid access (sell it at your rate)
  • All copyrights with Authors
  • Network with colleagues, blog to a wider industry
  • Notify the world of your work with Tweets and LinkedIn portals
